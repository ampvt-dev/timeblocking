# Blockwise — Brand Kit v1

## Brand foundation
Blockwise is a native macOS and Windows time blocking app that helps people give their day a clear shape.

Brand promise: Make time for what matters.
Personality: intentional, capable, warm, direct.
Write the product name Blockwise in prose and blockwise in the logo.

## Logo
Use the orange B symbol with a central forward triangle and lowercase charcoal wordmark as the primary signature. Keep the symbol geometry consistent across all applications. Prefer flat orange; reserve subtle fold shading for large promotional artwork.
Use a white signature on charcoal backgrounds and a solid charcoal signature when color is unavailable.
Clear space: leave at least one quarter of the symbol height around every side.
Suggested minimum widths: horizontal logo 140 px; standalone symbol 24 px. Small menu and tray icons require a separately simplified monochrome rendering.
Do not stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows, or change the symbol's internal proportions.

## Color tokens
| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Ember | #E6511B | Brand symbol, focus indicators, selected accents |
| Charcoal | #202426 | Primary text, dark surfaces |
| Ivory | #FAF8F3 | Main light background |
| Peach | #F7D7C7 | Quiet emphasis and time block fills |
| Slate | #737B82 | Supporting graphics and secondary decoration |

Use charcoal text on ivory or peach. Use white text on charcoal. Treat orange primarily as an accent; assess text contrast before using it behind small text. Status and task categories should also have labels or icons.
Suggested visual balance: 70% ivory, 20% charcoal, 10% orange and supporting colors.

## Typography
Proposed brand typeface: Inter.
Headlines: Semibold, sentence case, concise.
Body: Regular, comfortable spacing.
Labels and controls: Medium.
Desktop product interfaces may use the platform system typeface to retain a native feel, while marketing uses Inter consistently.

## App icon
Orange background, centered white B, generous padding. Preserve the forward triangle. Prepare separate macOS and Windows export packages from an approved vector master; the concept board is a visual reference, not an installable icon package.

## Voice
Short, helpful, calm. Encourage deliberate choices without guilt or exaggerated productivity promises.
Examples: “Plan your day.” “Start focus time.” “Move this block.” “Make room for a break.”
Avoid: “Crush every minute”, urgency-driven messages, or claims that imply unfinished work is failure.

## Graphic language
Use the logo's angular cuts sparingly in large background shapes. Time blocks use softly rounded corners and generous space. Keep the orange accent reserved for the most important focus or selected state.

## Marketing copy
Headline: Make time for what matters.
Description: Give your day a clear shape with time blocking for macOS and Windows.
Primary action: Plan your day.

## Deliverable status
This kit establishes a creative direction. The accompanying generated board is a raster concept. Editable vector logos, exact font files, and platform icon exports are subsequent production assets and are not included in this concept kit.
